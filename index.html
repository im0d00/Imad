01 About

Who I Am

I'm Aimad Ul Islam, a Computer Science graduate from the University of Peshawar, Pakistan, with a growing specialisation in cybersecurity, network security, and security operations. My journey began with a fascination for how systems communicate — and how they can be compromised, defended, and hardened.

I hold Cisco Networking Academy certifications in Networking Essentials and Introduction to Cybersecurity, earned the Cisco Learn-A-Thon 2026 Achievement Award, and completed a Mastercard Cybersecurity Job Simulation covering phishing analysis and incident triage. I practice packet analysis with Wireshark and maintain an active cybersecurity home lab.

Alongside security studies, I co-founded Lizzdo.com — a digital technology platform — building 7+ years of expertise in AI tools, automation, remote operations, and international client management.

My goal is a career in threat detection, security research, and digital forensics, within SOC environments, penetration testing firms, or security research organisations.
