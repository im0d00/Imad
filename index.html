01 // About

Who I Am

I'm Aimad Ul Islam, a Computer Science graduate from the University of Peshawar, Pakistan, with a growing specialisation in cybersecurity, network security, and security operations. My journey into cybersecurity began with a fascination for how systems communicate — and how they can be compromised, defended, and hardened.

I hold Cisco Networking Academy certifications in Networking Essentials and Introduction to Cybersecurity, earned my Cisco Learn-A-Thon 2026 Achievement Award, and completed a Mastercard Cybersecurity Job Simulation covering phishing analysis and incident triage. I actively practice packet analysis with Wireshark and maintain a personal cybersecurity home lab.

Alongside my security studies, I co-founded Lizzdo.com — a digital technology platform serving international clients — where I've spent 7+ years building expertise in AI-powered tools, automation systems, remote team management, and technology product development.

My goal is to build a career at the intersection of threat detection, security research, and digital forensics, working within SOC environments or security-focused organisations that value curiosity, precision, and continuous learning.
